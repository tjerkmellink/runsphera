# LLM coach voice

> A personalized one-to-two-sentence coach line at the top of the dashboard, generated by your chosen LLM (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or Google Gemini) from your runner profile, recent activity, and today's rules-engine decision.

## Provider choice

Three providers are pluggable from Settings → Coach voice; switching never clears the `llm_cache` table, so previously-generated coach messages stay readable even after you change provider.

| Provider | Default model | Free? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | `claude-opus-4-7` | Pay-as-you-go after $5 starter credit | Sharpest voice; uses prompt caching for cheaper recall on stable profile context |
| OpenAI | `gpt-4o-mini` | Pay-as-you-go after $5 starter credit | ~€0.10/year at one call/day; capable enough for the coach use case |
| Gemini | `gemini-2.5-flash` | **Truly free** on AI Studio (1500 req/day) | The only ongoing-free option — get a key at aistudio.google.com |

The active provider is stored in `app_settings.llm_provider`; each provider's key in its own column (`anthropic_api_key`, `openai_api_key`, `gemini_api_key`), so switching providers never clobbers another's saved key. Provider modules live in `running_coach/llm/providers/_<name>.py`, dispatched through `running_coach/llm/connector.py`.

## Purpose

The rules engine (GO / CAUTION / EASY / REST / DONE) gives correct decisions but a flat tone. The LLM layer wraps those decisions in warm, specific language — referencing the runner's actual data (VDOT, last week's km, diary mood, days to race) so the daily banner feels like a coach talking *to them*, not a status indicator. On days without a scheduled workout (gap days, pre-plan-start, rest days), the coach still speaks, drawing on recent runs and diary entries.

A second surface, the **post-run reaction**, gives the runner a short coach-level debrief on every completed activity when they open it in the run-detail modal. Same persona, scoped to one run; cached per activity; regenerates when the diary note or Garmin feel/RPE changes.

A third surface, the **weekly recap**, anchors the runner to the weekly arc with a Sunday-evening-style debrief on the dashboard. Covers the most-recently-completed Mon–Sun week. Same persona, structured as a 3–4 sentence arc (what landed → observation → next-week setup); cached per ISO week; regenerates on sync and on any diary write.

## How it works (user view)

### Daily banner

- The banner sits at the top of the dashboard (`/`), above the today's-note editor.
- When a planned workout exists, the banner shows the **GO / CAUTION / EASY / REST** icon and a colored left border matching the rules-engine decision. The voiced line replaces (or augments) the rules-engine headline.
- Once that workout is logged, the banner flips to **DONE** ("Logged") and the line becomes a debrief on the run rather than a prescription — see [Sessions that already happened](#sessions-that-already-happened).
- When there's no planned workout, the banner switches to a neutral "check-in" style — no icon, accent-colored left border, just the voiced line.
- A **↻ button** to the right of the message regenerates today's line on demand (bypassing the cache).
- Without a Claude API key configured, the banner falls back to the rules-engine headline. Nothing breaks.

### Post-run reaction

- A small accent-bordered strip at the **top of the run-detail modal**, generated the first time you open a run.
- **Adaptive length** — a one-line take for routine easy runs / shakeouts, a 2-4 sentence debrief for quality sessions, long runs, races, PRs, or first time at a distance.
- **↻ button** regenerates on demand.
- Silent when LLM isn't configured (no key, network error) — the rest of the modal renders normally.

### Weekly recap

- A card on the dashboard between the daily banner and Today's note, headed by a "Last week" pill and the date range (e.g. "Mon 04 May – Sun 10 May 2026").
- **3–4 sentences** following a deliberate arc: what landed → the honest observation → next week's headline focus.
- **Always the most-recently-completed Mon–Sun**: the rule is simple — today's in-progress day is never part of the recap window. On Sunday you're reading the recap of the week that ended last Sunday; on Monday morning the new week becomes available.
- **↻ button** regenerates on demand.
- Silent if there's truly nothing to summarise (no plan in that week + no runs + no diary).

## How it works (implementation)

### Modules

- [running_coach/llm/connector.py](../running_coach/llm/connector.py) — thin Anthropic SDK wrapper. Handles missing API keys, typed exception handling (`AuthenticationError`, `RateLimitError`, `APIError`), returns `LLMResult` or `None`. Defaults to `claude-opus-4-7` with adaptive thinking + `effort: "medium"`.
- [running_coach/llm/prompts.py](../running_coach/llm/prompts.py) — three system prompts (`SYSTEM_PROMPT` for the daily banner, `RUN_REACTION_SYSTEM_PROMPT` for the per-run debrief, `WEEKLY_RECAP_SYSTEM_PROMPT` for the Sunday wrap-up), one shared `profile_block(...)` (cached runner identity), and three request-block builders (`request_block`, `run_reaction_request_block`, `weekly_recap_request_block`).
- [running_coach/llm/context.py](../running_coach/llm/context.py) — pulls the runner profile, recent runs, and diary entries from SQLite. Four builders: `build_profile_context(conn)`, `build_request_context(conn, recommendation, todays_workout)`, `build_run_reaction_context(conn, activity_id)`, and `build_weekly_recap_context(conn, today=None)`. Plus the small `target_recap_week(today)` helper that picks the most-recently-completed Mon–Sun.
- [running_coach/llm/commentary.py](../running_coach/llm/commentary.py) — three public entry points:
  - `daily_coach_message(conn, recommendation, todays_workout, *, force_refresh=False)` — daily banner, day-keyed cache.
  - `run_reaction(conn, activity_id, *, force_refresh=False)` — per-activity coach debrief, cached under `run_reaction:{activity_id}` in `llm_cache`.
  - `weekly_recap(conn, *, force_refresh=False, today=None)` — last week's recap, cached under `weekly_recap:YYYY-Www`. Returns `{text, week_label, week_start_iso, week_end_iso, week_key}` so the template can render the header.
  - `daily_coach_generated_at(conn)` / `weekly_recap_generated_at(conn)` / `run_reaction_generated_at(conn, activity_id)` — local HH:MM the cached message was written. Powers the editorial byline footer in the Tempo theme (see [design-system.md](design-system.md)).
  - Plus `invalidate_today(...)`, `invalidate_run_reaction(...)`, and `invalidate_weekly_recap(...)` for explicit cache drops.

### Templates

- [_coach_message.html](../running_coach/web/templates/_coach_message.html) — the daily headline + ↻ regenerate button. Used both inline in [index.html](../running_coach/web/templates/index.html) and as the HTMX swap target.
- [_run_reaction.html](../running_coach/web/templates/_run_reaction.html) — the post-run debrief strip + ↻ regenerate button. Included at the top of [_run_detail.html](../running_coach/web/templates/_run_detail.html); also the HTMX swap target.
- [_weekly_recap.html](../running_coach/web/templates/_weekly_recap.html) — the "Last week" card + ↻ regenerate button. Included on the dashboard between the daily banner and Today's note; also the HTMX swap target.

Tempo theme adds editorial pull-quote versions of each — see [design-system.md](design-system.md#editorial-byline-trio-llm-voice-surfaces). The refresh routes select the per-theme partial based on `app_settings.get_theme(conn)` so the HTMX swap preserves the editorial layout.

### Routes

- `GET /` — calls `daily_coach_message` and `weekly_recap` and passes the results to the dashboard.
- `POST /coach-message/refresh` — regenerates the daily banner with `force_refresh=True`, returns just the headline partial.
- `POST /weekly-recap/refresh` — regenerates the weekly recap with `force_refresh=True`, returns the recap-card partial.
- `GET /runs/{activity_id}` — calls `run_reaction(conn, activity_id)` lazily and passes the result into the run-detail modal.
- `POST /runs/{activity_id}/reaction/refresh` — regenerates the reaction with `force_refresh=True`, returns the partial.

### DB

- `llm_cache` table — three key patterns:
  - `daily_coach:YYYY-MM-DD` — the daily banner, one entry per day.
  - `run_reaction:<activity_id>` — the post-run debrief, one entry per completed activity. Persists indefinitely (objective run data doesn't change) until invalidated.
  - `weekly_recap:YYYY-Www` — the Sunday-evening wrap, one entry per ISO week. Once a week passes the cache entry is effectively permanent (no scheduled cleanup), so old recaps accumulate slowly — that's fine, they're tiny.
- Stored fields: generated text, model id, usage JSON.
- Reads from: `app_settings` (name, About-you, API key), `training_status`, `training_readiness`, `activities`, `diary_entries`, `plans`, `plan_workouts`, plus per-activity `plan_workouts` (matched) and `diary_entries` (linked) for run reactions.

### Data flow

```
Garmin sync → SQLite ──┬─→ context.build_profile_context ─→ profile_block (cached)
                       │                                                   ↓
                       └─→ context.build_request_context ─→ request_block ─┴→ Claude API
                                                                              ↓
plan_coach.daily_recommendation ─→ recommendation dict ──┘                  text response

                                                                              ↓

```

### Sessions that already happened

Once today's planned workout is matched to an activity, the request block switches from prescriptive to observational, and it has to do so *consistently* — the coach reads the whole block and will invent a story to reconcile any part of it that disagrees.

Three things flip together:

1. **The workout header** becomes `TODAY'S WORKOUT (already completed TODAY, Thu 2026-07-30 — comment on the run, do not prescribe)`, with the date spelled out. The system prompt pairs this with an explicit "it was today, not yesterday".
2. **The rules engine reports `DONE`** instead of GO/CAUTION/EASY (see [dashboard.md](dashboard.md#readiness-coaching-the-hero)), so no swap suggestion reaches the prompt. Its readiness / ACWR chips are relabelled *"State right now (measured AFTER the run)"* with a line stating they describe what the session cost.
3. **`ACTUAL EXECUTION BY PHASE`** appears for structured workouts, so adherence is judged on the active block rather than the warmup-dragged total average.

Without (2) the block contradicted itself: a completed interval session sitting above `Status: EASY — swap today's quality session for an easy run, or rest entirely`. The system prompt tells the coach to defer to the rules engine, so the only reading that satisfies both is that the intervals happened on an *earlier* day — which is exactly what it wrote ("Je intervallen gisteren…" on 2026-07-30, for a run logged at 12:25 that same day). The lesson generalises: a lagging metric presented next to a completed action needs its lag stated, or the model will move the action instead.

One related wrinkle in the same block: the rules engine flags ACWR as "drifting high" from 1.30 while Garmin still labels 1.30 OPTIMAL. Both wordings are intentional (the app applies the stricter threshold), so the raw line is attributed — `ACWR: 1.30 (Garmin status: OPTIMAL)` — rather than reading as the data contradicting itself.

### Prompt caching

The Anthropic API caches by *prefix*. The render order is `tools → system → messages`, so we structure the prompt to keep stable bytes first:

1. **System prompt** (persona, scope, format) — `cache_control: ephemeral`. Same across every call.
2. **First user message: profile block** — `cache_control: ephemeral`. Same across calls within a session; changes when name / VDOT / plan / recent runs / diary roll over.
3. **Second user message: request block** — uncached. Today's specific data + rules-engine decision.

This means a fresh call costs full input + output tokens; subsequent calls within ~5 minutes pay only for the request block + output.

### Cache invalidation

**Daily banner** (`daily_coach:YYYY-MM-DD`) is dropped:

- On Garmin sync (`POST /ingest`), after new activities land.
- On workout swap (`POST /plan-workouts/{id}/apply-swap`), since today's workout changed shape.
- On About-you save (`POST /settings/about`), since the profile block contents changed.
- On diary edit / backfill (`POST /diary/new`, `POST /diary/entries/{id}`), since recent diary feeds the profile.
- On location save / clear (`POST /settings/location*`), since the weather block changes.
- On calendar favorite / unfavorite, since saved races feed the upcoming-context surface.
- Manually via the ↻ button on the banner.

**Run reaction** (`run_reaction:<activity_id>`) is dropped:

- On per-activity diary save (`POST /runs/{activity_id}/diary`).
- On Garmin feel/RPE refresh during `_hydrate_garmin_feel` — but **only when the values actually changed**, to avoid burning a regeneration on every modal open.
- Manually via the ↻ button on the run-reaction strip.

**Weekly recap** (`weekly_recap:YYYY-Www`) is dropped for the *currently-targeted* week:

- On Garmin sync (`POST /ingest`), since new activities may land in the recap window.
- On diary backfill (`POST /diary/new`), edit (`POST /diary/entries/{id}`), and per-activity save (`POST /runs/{id}/diary`) — any of these can change a diary entry that falls inside last week.
- Manually via the ↻ button on the recap card.

Older weeks' cached recaps stay untouched — they describe a fixed period and won't change.

## Configuration

| What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropic API key | `/settings` → Claude card, or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` env var | DB-stored key overrides env. Cleared from Settings falls back to env value. |
| About-you note | `/settings` → About you card | Optional. When set, surfaces in the cached profile block as "WHAT THE RUNNER WANTS YOU TO KNOW — treat as ground truth". |
| Model | `DEFAULT_MODEL = "claude-opus-4-7"` in [connector.py](../running_coach/llm/connector.py) | Edit to downgrade for cost. Adaptive thinking + `effort: "medium"` set per call. |

## Known limitations

- **Hallucinated specifics still possible.** The system prompt forbids inventing numbers, but the model can occasionally cite a metric not in the data. If you see one, regenerate via ↻; if it keeps happening for the same value, the issue is the prompt, not the model.
- **Day-of-week is hard for LLMs.** Once observed: the weekly recap referred to "Friday's easy run" when the run was actually on Saturday. LLMs are unreliable at deriving weekday from bare ISO dates. Every date the recap, daily-coach, run-reaction, and profile prompts emit is now pre-rendered with the weekday via `_with_dow()` in [prompts.py](../running_coach/llm/prompts.py) (`'2026-05-09'` → `'Sat 2026-05-09'`). If a future prompt builder includes a new date field, run it through that helper.
- **No streaming.** The banner waits for the full response before rendering. With Opus + adaptive thinking, that's ~3–8s on a fresh call, ~1–2s on a cached call. Acceptable for once-a-day, but the ↻ button feels slow.
- **No multi-day awareness in voice.** Each day's message is generated independently. Claude doesn't know what it said yesterday, so it can repeat phrasing or themes across days. Not yet a problem in practice.
- **Cache scoped to one runner.** The cache key has no user id because this is a single-user app. If multi-user support ever ships, the cache key needs scoping.
- **Adherence not yet in the prompt.** The recent runs and rules-engine decision are present, but week-level adherence stats (the new `done/planned` badge) aren't surfaced to the LLM yet. Easy add when the plan has been running long enough for it to matter.

## Verification

1. Open `/settings`, confirm Claude card shows "● Configured" (or "● Not configured" — both are valid states).
2. With a key configured: open `/`. The banner should show a 1–2 sentence message that references at least one specific data point from your last 7 days (a run, a mood, readiness score, etc.). Click ↻ — the message should regenerate, with the same data grounding but different phrasing.
3. Without a key (clear it from Settings): refresh `/`. The banner should fall back to the rules-engine headline (e.g. "Well recovered — go execute today's tempo.") without errors.
4. Save the About-you note with "I'm currently managing a left-knee niggle." Click ↻ on the banner. The next generation should reference the niggle if today's workout is anything quality-flavored.
5. **Post-run reaction**: open any recent run from the dashboard. A short reaction should appear at the top of the modal — one line for routine runs, 2-4 sentences for quality / long / race / PR efforts. Click ↻ — it should regenerate with similar grounding but different phrasing.
6. **Reaction invalidation on diary**: open the same run, save a diary note ("legs felt heavy in the second half"), reopen the modal. The reaction should regenerate and reference the note.
7. **No-LLM state**: clear the Claude key from Settings, open a run. The reaction strip should be absent; the rest of the modal renders normally.
8. **Weekly recap**: open the dashboard. A "Last week" card should appear between the daily banner and Today's note, with a 3–4 sentence reflection on the Mon–Sun period that most recently ended. Click ↻ — regenerates inline (one Claude call, ~5–7 s). Save a diary entry that falls inside last week, reload — the recap should regenerate on the next dashboard view.

## Future improvements

- **Per-day-tile commentary** — extend the LLM to add a muted one-liner under each day-tile in the plan view, explaining *why* this workout sits here in this week.
- **Week intro paragraph** — on the plan page, a paragraph above each week summarizing what's coming and how it builds on the prior week.
- **Streaming responses** — switch the connector + ↻ button to stream tokens via SSE for a more responsive feel.
- **Weekly digest** — once a week, Claude could write a longer reflective summary referencing the diary entries.
- **Adherence in the prompt** — surface the per-week `done/missed/upcoming` numbers in the request block once there's a few weeks of real data to talk about.
